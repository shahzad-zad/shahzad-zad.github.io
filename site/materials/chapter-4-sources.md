## Textbook corrections

XI printed p.87: for a solid cylinder M=2 kg and R=0.5 m, I=0.25 kg m², not 0.5. For the thick cylinder M=3 kg, inner radius 0.4 m, outer radius 0.6 m, I=0.78 kg m², not 1.2. Circular satellite motion is continual gravitational free fall; it does not require alignment with Earth's rotation or a force cancelling gravity.

XII printed p.65 gives conflicting directions in the electron-beam procedure. Magnetic deflection requires a component of velocity perpendicular to the field; parallel velocity has zero magnetic force. Notes use crossed perpendicular fields for velocity selection and a separate perpendicular magnetic field for curvature. Toroid turn count N is total turns; n in the long-solenoid expression is turns per unit length.
